# Bloom — Investment Analysis for Women

## Vision
Mobile-first investment app designed specifically for women investors. Warm, approachable interface that demystifies investing through Pansy, Bloom's expert guide who explains everything in plain girlfriend-tone language.

## Design
- **Primary:** `--primary: 153 23% 36% (sage green #4A6B4E)` — grounding, trustworthy
- **Accent:** `--accent: 45 50% 54% (soft gold #C9A84C)` — premium highlights, Pansy picks
- **Background:** `--background: 45 56% 98% (cream white #FAF8F4)` — warm, soft
- **Foreground:** `--foreground: 153 25% 25% (dark sage)` — readable text
- **Muted:** `--muted: 45 30% 92% (light cream)` — subtle backgrounds

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (display, editorial credibility) + DM Sans (body, geometric warmth)
Style: 1960s editorial meets organic skincare boutique — warm authority with gentle confidence

## Features
- **Onboarding:** 4-step flow capturing experience, goals, risk tolerance ✅
- **Home:** Personalized greeting, live market summary, Pansy's daily picks, market movers ✅
- **Stock Analysis:** TradingView charts, Pansy's girlfriend-tone analysis, real-time news ✅
- **Discover:** Filterable stocks/ETFs/mutual funds with sparkline charts ✅
- **Brokers:** Featured and non-affiliate broker listings with tracking ✅
- **Subscription:** Free vs Pro plans with PayPal integration ✅
- **Admin Dashboard:** Protected analytics, user management, revenue tracking ✅
- **Pansy Popups:** Context-aware expert guidance (first visit, returning user, market tips) ✅

All core features complete. Ready for API keys and testing.
